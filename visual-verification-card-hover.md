# Service and vacancy card hover verification

The homepage service carousel and job-search vacancy list were verified at desktop (1280x720) and mobile (375x812). The new service-card hover/focus treatment adds lift, image emphasis, accent color, and arrow movement without altering the mobile touch layout. Vacancy rows retain their compact editorial structure while gaining a subtle lift, border, accent marker, title emphasis, and action-arrow movement on hover/focus. Touch media disables hover lift, and reduced-motion users receive no transitions.

Automated verification passed with 40 test files and 66 tests, followed by a successful production build.
