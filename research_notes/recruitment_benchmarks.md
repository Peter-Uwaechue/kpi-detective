# Recruitment benchmark reference notes

The planned animated counters must be presented as **industry reference points**, not as Willers Solutions Limited performance claims. They should include a visible qualification and a source link.

| Metric | Reference value | Scope and caveat | Source |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Offer acceptance rate | 69.3% | Crosschq describes this as an average offer-acceptance rate; its article also defines the metric as the share of candidates accepting a formal, unique job offer. It is not Nigeria-specific or a Willers result. | https://www.crosschq.com/blog/time-to-hire-offer-acceptance-rate |
| Applicant-to-interview conversion | 20% | CareerPlug’s 2022 Recruiting Metrics report states that the average applicant-to-interview conversion rate rose to 20% in 2021. It is historic, industry-wide benchmark data rather than a current Willers metric. | https://www.careerplug.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/2022-Recruiting-Metrics-Report.pdf |

Recommended on-site qualification: “Industry benchmark references. Figures vary by role, sector, hiring conditions, and location; they are not Willers Solutions performance results.”
